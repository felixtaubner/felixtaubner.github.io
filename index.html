Felix Taubner

PhD student in Computer Science, University of Toronto

I work on 4D generative models with a focus on digital humans, advised by David Lindell.

Recently, I completed a research internship at Meta Reality Labs (Pittsburgh) under the supervision of Michael Zollhoefer. Prior to this, I was an AI Research Scientist at LG Electronics, working on 3D human face reconstruction and animation.

I completed my BSc. (Mechanical Engineering) and MSc. (Robotics) at ETH Zurich, specializing in computer vision and control. Previously, I conducted research at the Autonomous Systems Lab at ETH under the supervision of Roland Siegwart, and at the Toronto Intelligent Systems Lab at U of T under the supervision of Igor Gilitschenski.

My research interests are 3D computer vision, 4D generative AI and digital human animation.
